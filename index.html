I am a PhD candidate in Statistics and Data Science at Cornell University, advised by Sumanta Basu and Martin T. Wells. I expect to complete my PhD in May 2027.

My research focuses on Bayesian methods, conformal prediction, and time series. Recently, I have studied uncertainty quantification for next-generation reservoir computing (NGRC), including how Bayesian and conformal prediction intervals behave in high-dimensional and temporally dependent settings. I am a recipient of the Artificial Intelligence for Sustainability (AI4S) NRT Fellowship, which will support my doctoral research in Spring 2027.

I have also developed Bayesian methods for regression and variable selection in short-term longitudinal omics data, with applications to biomarker discovery.

Outside of my doctoral research, I have worked in quantitative finance at Quantbot Technologies and in nuclear forensics at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. Before Cornell, I received bachelor's degrees in Statistics and Economics from NC State University.
